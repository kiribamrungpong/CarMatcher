Useful Tips

A quick guide to using Car Matcher, with sample pictures from the site. Read as much or as little as you like. Click "More details" to see more. You can start searching at any time: Find a Car.

Step 1: Enter your maximum budget

Type the most you want to spend, using numbers only (for example, 30000), or click one of the popular budgets to fill it in. Car Matcher only shows cars priced at or under this amount.

More details

Commas and a dollar sign are OK too, so 30,000 and $30000 both work.

If you type something that isn't a number, or a number that's 0 or less, a red message under the box tells you how to fix it. Your answers stay filled in.

Figure 1. The budget box

Step 2: Choose a vehicle type

  • Sedan: four doors and a trunk. Good for commuting.
  • Coupe: two doors and a sporty shape. Fun to drive, less room.
  • SUV: taller, with more room for passengers and cargo.
  • Truck: an open bed for hauling and towing.
More details

Pick one type. If you're not sure, try one, then switch types on the results page to compare.

Figure 2. Vehicle type choices

Step 3: Read your results

Click Find My Car. You'll see every car on our list that fits, cheapest first, with how much each one is under your budget.

More details

To change your search, use Refine Your Search under the results and click Update Results. Your list is replaced right away.

If nothing matches, the page tells you the cheapest car of that type and gives you links to try a different type with the same budget.

Figure 3. Search results

Going back and starting over

  • Back to search takes you to the form with your answers still filled in.
  • Start Over clears your answers, but it asks you first, so you can change your mind.

Good to know

  • Prices are approximate starting prices for each 2025 model's base trim. Taxes, dealer fees, and options will add to what you pay.
  • Car Matcher is free, needs no account, and doesn't save your answers.
